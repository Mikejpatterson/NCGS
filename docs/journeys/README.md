- [3. A golfer gets to their group, scores, checks the board and contests, loses signal, reloads, and carries on from another phone](3-golfer-scores-a-round/README.md)
- [4. A member follows a two-day trip and the season table](4-trip-and-season/README.md)

- [6. The organiser confirms contests, checks cards, finalises, then corrects a mistake](6-organiser-finishes-a-round/README.md)
