# UX review, 26 October 2026: screens to review

The owner keeps finding screens that aren't user friendly. This is a structured walk-through of the Westport, breakfast-draw and Groups situations, captured from the invented-fixture preview (`node tools/ux-capture.js <preview url> docs/ux-review/shots`). There are no real members here.

**Who does what.** Claude reviews the **process**: what the person is trying to do, the steps, what's missing or surplus, and what goes wrong on the day. Codex reviews the **visual** side: hierarchy, clarity, words, consistency with the approved design (pine, ivory, the crest, champagne only on pine), touch and readability outdoors or across a breakfast room. Each of us reviews **independently** first, then Claude merges both into one prioritised report for the owner.

**For each screen, ask:**
- Who is looking, and what are they trying to do?
- Is the main thing obvious within a second?
- What could go?
- What's missing?
- What happens after a wrong tap?
- Does it look and read right next to the screens before and after it?

**People:**
- **organiser**: Michael, with the PIN.
- **player**: someone scoring for their group on the course.
- **member**: someone following or waiting.

Sizes: `phone` is 390×844 and `ipad` is 1024×1366. Long pages are captured full length.
